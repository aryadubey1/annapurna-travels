Our story

Luxury journey with care, since 1989.

It started with one bus.

In 1989, in Bhopal, Mr. Narendra Ratnawat bought a single bus. He drove it himself, maintained it, took the bookings and greeted every passenger. For him it was never just transport. It was a journey filled with care.

Word of mouth turned into loyalty. One bus became five, then ten. Today, under Mr. Naman Ratnawat, Annapurna runs 100+ vehicles with 200+ professionals, and the soul is the same.

From Bhopal’s narrow streets to India’s busy corporate avenues, Annapurna earned the trust of top businesses like Procter & Gamble, Crompton and Welspun with punctual, professional and comfortable transport.

Reputed schools, including The Sanskaar Valley School, trust us with safe and reliable daily commutes for their children.

Today we keep growing and modernising: GPS-enabled buses, trained staff and a customer-first approach. But every journey should still be more than movement. It should be an experience of care, comfort and class.

This is not just our story. It is our legacy in motion.
